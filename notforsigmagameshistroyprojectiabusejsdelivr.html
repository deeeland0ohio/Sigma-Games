New World: Pros

  • The New World has load bearing animals, such as horses and donkeys.
  • Horses allowed for cattle ranching, hauling objects, buffalo hunting, and much more, completely transforming most native cultures.
  • The New World also has livestock such as pigs, chickens, sheep, and goats. Pigs and cows provided a readily available food supply for the natives and settlers.

New World: Cons

  • Most Old World food didn't arrive in the New World.
  • Language barriers with the natives made it hard to communicate.
  • The New World didn't have the force of their full army from back in Spain.
  • Many Natives died due to diseases that they had never encountered before.
  • Natives disliked the New World and would attack them regularly.

Old World: Pros

  • Columbus was able to take possession of the people on the islands.
  • Europeans benefited from corn, due to its ability to grow fast.
  • They were able to get their hands of gold from their Native slaves.
  • Europeans were able to transfer around 10 million Africans to America which balanced out the population in the New World.
  • The Spanish established ranchos with cattle, which provided lots of meat.

Old World: Cons

  • African diseases also killed the Europeans too!
  • The Natives disliked how the Old World was treating them!
  • Europeans and people from the Old World had trouble communicating with the Natives.
  • When the Europeans first entered the New World, they feared the Natives because they outnumbered them.
